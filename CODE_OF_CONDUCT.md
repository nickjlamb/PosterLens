# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is acting in good faith. Argue about the code, not the
person writing it.

## Standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Taking responsibility, apologising, and learning from mistakes
- Focusing on what is best for the people using PosterLens
- Being patient with people who are new to Swift, to iOS, or to the domain

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions
- Anything else a reasonable professional would recognise as inappropriate

## Scope

This applies in the repository — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages — and
anywhere someone is officially representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to **info@pharmatools.ai**. Reports are handled privately and
the reporter's identity is not disclosed.

The maintainer will respond in a way proportionate to the incident: a private word, a public
correction, a temporary block, or a permanent ban. Judgement is exercised in good faith and
the maintainer's decision is final.
